**Who:**
A tournament open to all members of the [AGA](https://www.usgo.org/)!
Joining the AGA typically costs $30; if you're joining as a Maine resident, contact us to get a discount code.
The tournament is free for new AGA members.  
(All attendees will be credited **50% off** registration for the [New England Open](https://wmgc.massgo.org/ne-open-2026/), Sept 4-6!)

**What:**
Three rounds of games, AGA rules,
20 minutes plus 20 seconds Fischer time.
Everyone is eligible to play all rounds, and all rounds will count toward prizes at the end of the day.  
**Maine State Championship:**
A knockout tournament for up to 8 of the highest ranked Maine residents.
Players get knocked out will play Open games in subsequent rounds.
The winner will be crowned Maine State Champion.  
**Maine Open:**
A [McMahon tournament](https://senseis.xmp.net/?McMahonPairing) open to all AGA members.
As much as possible, players will play even games against opponents of a similar rank.
